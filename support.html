Chessapeake Support

Need help with the app, a purchase, a saved game, or a position that feels off? Send a note and include any details that will help us reproduce it.

For fastest support, include your device model, iOS version, app version, and any PGN or FEN that shows the issue.

Contact

Email dseppi@gmail.com for Chessapeake support.

Helpful details

  • What you were doing when the issue happened.
  • Whether it happens every time or only sometimes.
  • Screenshots, crash details, PGN, FEN, or move history when relevant.
  • Whether the issue involves purchases, saved games, training progress, or analysis.

Privacy

Support messages are handled according to the Chessapeake Privacy Policy.
