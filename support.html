Support

How can we help?

Chessapeake is built to be calm, understandable, and fun. If something feels confusing or broken, send a note and include the details below so we can help quickly.

Contact

Email support

Email dseppi@gmail.com with the subject line Chessapeake Support.

Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are welcome.

Common questions

How do I restore purchases?

Open the Plus or purchase screen in Chessapeake and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are handled through Apple StoreKit and your App Store account.

How do subscriptions work?

Chessapeake Plus uses App Store subscriptions. You can manage, cancel, or review renewal status in your Apple account subscription settings.

The coach seems wrong

Chess is complicated. If a review or coach comment seems off, send the PGN, FEN, or screenshot. Those examples help us investigate concrete positions.

Can I play offline?

Core chess play and local coaching features are designed to work on device. Some App Store purchase and restore actions require Apple services.

Good bug reports

  • What were you doing right before the problem happened?
  • Did it happen once, or can you repeat it?
  • What opponent, persona, time control, or lesson were you using?
  • If it was a chess-position issue, include the PGN or FEN if possible.

Privacy and diagnostics

Chessapeake does not automatically upload diagnostics. Share only the screenshots, PGN, FEN, or device details you choose to include in an email. Never send an App Store password, payment card number, or purchase receipt.

Open-source software

Chessapeake includes Stockfish 19 under GPLv3-or-later. Download the exact matching source package, including the license, NNUE network, build flags, checksums, and Chessapeake bridge modifications.

Availability and known issues

Release-blocking issues are published here when confirmed. If a purchase is unavailable, core offline chess remains playable; use Restore Purchases after Apple services recover. If engine review stops because the device is warm, the game remains saved and review can be resumed after the device cools.
